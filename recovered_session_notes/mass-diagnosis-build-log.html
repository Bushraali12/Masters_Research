PART I

The build, in order

Each stage is one decision. The number under it is the pooled lesion-level AUC on the official test set unless stated otherwise, and the chip is what that stage was worth against the stage before it.

01

Data foundation and the reporting units

Starting point

The CBIS-DDSM mass subset: 1,696 ROI crops, 912 benign and 784 malignant, resolving to 1,005 lesions, 932 breasts and 892 patients. This is the standard benchmark subset other ROI papers report on, which makes the work directly comparable rather than idiosyncratic.

Four reporting units were defined up front. ROI → lesion by mean score (the CC and MLO crops show the same object). Lesion → breast → patient by mean score and max label. BI-RADS aggregates by max.

Official test cohort378 ROI · 361 mammograms · 223 lesions · 210 breasts · 201 patients
02

Split integrity, audited before anything was trained

Foundation

Every split is patient-grouped. A later full audit confirmed zero straddling at mammogram, breast, lesion and patient level simultaneously — so the 71 mammograms carrying more than one abnormality can never leak across the train/test line.

Crop integrity was checked separately: 0 unreadable files, 0 crops contaminated by the well-known CBIS-DDSM crop/mask ordering bug, all 512×512, median 116 distinct intensity levels.

03

Stage 1 — segmentation: DS-Attn-UNet with ASPP

First model

A deeply-supervised attention U-Net with an ASPP bottleneck, trained patient-blind. Masks used downstream always come from a model that never saw that patient. An ablation attributed 86% of the Dice gain to the ASPP bottleneck.

Provenance was verified: Dice on training-fold images 0.898 versus 0.905 held out — no memorisation.

Dice 0.90655-model average on test · per-fold 0.8972 ± 0.0040
04

Baseline floor — handcrafted radiomic features

Classifier v0

Shape, texture and intensity features from the ROI, classified conventionally. This established how much of the problem is not solvable by traditional descriptors.

0.6807lesion AUCfloor
05

First CNN — single tight crop, DenseNet-121

Classifier v1

ImageNet-pretrained DenseNet-121, one 512×512 lesion-centred crop, plain global average pooling. No mask, no context, no auxiliary heads. This is the mask-blind control that every later gain is measured against.

0.8092lesion AUC+0.1285 over radiomics
06

Training recipe: transfer learning, focal loss, auxiliary heads

Classifier v2

Progressive unfreezing (3 frozen epochs, then discriminative learning rates — 3e-5 backbone, 3e-4 head), focal loss γ=2 with class weighting, CLAHE, and three auxiliary heads predicting subtlety, mass_shape and mass_margins at weight 0.3. Four-fold test-time augmentation.

0.8715lesion AUC+0.0623
07

Two-stream input: tight crop plus wide context

The largest single gain

A second input branch carrying a wider crop around the lesion, giving the network the peritumoral region — margin, spiculation, surrounding architecture — alongside the lesion itself. Features from both streams concatenated before the head.

Subgroup analysis showed the wide stream helps most exactly where it should: circumscribed margins +0.0587, round shapes +0.0572, mid-subtlety lesions +0.0253.

0.8816lesion AUC, no mask+0.0724 over single crop
08

Novelty 1 — mask-weighted dual pooling

First contribution

The segmenter's own prediction becomes a pooling weight: w = 1 + 2·m. The feature map is pooled twice — once weighted, once unweighted — and both descriptors are concatenated. The lesion is emphasised without the surrounding tissue being discarded, and the module is parameter-free.

Verified not to be a mask-quality artifact: correlation between per-image Dice and classification gain was +0.0062 [−0.095, +0.107].

0.9043lesion AUC+0.0227 over the same model without the mask
09

Two-view combination, recognised for what it is

Reframing, not new work

Averaging the CC and MLO crops of one lesion is two-view combination — the same operation Shen et al. perform at breast level. Measured on this pipeline: ROI 0.8571 → lesion 0.8816.

+0.0245two-view gain — inside Shen's reported [+0.018, +0.064] band
10

Novelty 2 — per-BI-RADS decision layer

Second contribution

Rather than one global threshold, a separate threshold per BI-RADS category, all fitted jointly by multi-start coordinate ascent under a single cohort-level sensitivity floor. The mechanism is prevalence stratification: BI-RADS 3 is ~5% malignant, BI-RADS 5 is ~95%, so one cut-point cannot serve both.

Per-stratum floors are degenerate here — BI-RADS 2 contains exactly one malignant lesion — which is precisely why one cohort-level constraint is required.

85.7%lesion accuracy +5.83 points bootstrap +5.32 [+2.57, +8.11]
11

Protocol decision — official split as headline

Methodological

Both protocols were run. The official CBIS-DDSM split became the headline because it is what the comparable literature reports; patient-grouped 5-fold CV is reported as the secondary, and it comes in lower at every unit (−0.0035 to −0.0197), which is the honest direction.

An image-level split was measured on the identical model and data and inflated AUC by +0.089. That single number explains most of the implausible results in this literature, and it is reported.

CV 0.8885lesion AUC, secondary protocol−0.0158 vs official
12

Robustness and adversarial self-checks

Validation

Out-of-fold status of training predictions confirmed four independent ways. Mask provenance ≤0.007 AUC. BI-RADS 5 removed entirely — accuracy still 77.9–80.3%, so the result is not carried by the easy stratum. And a BI-RADS noise simulation: at 90% category accuracy the decision layer keeps 82% of its gain, at 85% it keeps 76%, and it never falls below break-even down to 50%.

13

Resolution and weight sharing

Latest gain

Tight stream 512 → 768, wide stream 384 → 512, and the two separate backbones collapsed into one shared-weight DenseNet-121. Parameters fell from 15.75M to 8.79M and every unit improved. Mammographic malignancy lives in margin detail, which downsampling destroys.

0.8932lesion AUC, no mask +0.011644% fewer parameters
14

Seven-variant pooling ablation

Today

Four attempts to obtain the mask's benefit without a mask at inference — GeM pooling, free attention pooling, mask-distilled attention, soft attention re-weighting, and an auxiliary segmentation decoder. Everything else held identical: same crops, split, schedule, augmentation, loss, TTA, and a parameter-matched 3072-d descriptor.

All four failed, and failed in proportion to how well they localised. Full table in Part III.

mask 0.9016best of sevenevery mask-free route below baseline
15

Where it stands

Current

Final model: two-stream DenseNet-121 with mask-weighted dual pooling, official split, plus the per-BI-RADS decision layer. Lesion AUC 0.9043, accuracy 85.7% at a 0.90 sensitivity floor.

The architecture search is closed. Seven pooling variants and eleven post-hoc strategies have been measured; the remaining work is one optional 5-fold ensemble run and the writing.

The finding

The better the network learns to localise the lesion, the worse it classifies. Sort the mask-free variants by IoU — 0.373, 0.686, 0.681, 0.745 — and the AUC loss deepens across the range: −0.0153, −0.0264, −0.0363, −0.0549.

The mechanism is not the pooling formula. Learning where costs capacity: free attention has no mask loss at all and still loses 0.0153 at n=1,098. Teaching where competes with learning what: every auxiliary localisation loss pulls the shared backbone away from the diagnostic objective, and seg_aux — the heaviest such loss, plus 2.5M extra parameters — is the worst variant in the table.

The mask variant pays neither cost. It injects known information directly into pooling with zero parameters and zero extra loss term. Localisation information helps only when it is supplied for free.

Independent corroboration

This reproduces a finding from the earlier cross-validation era, measured at different resolution under a different protocol: hard gating instead of soft weighting cost −0.11 AUC. Two independent confirmations that the diagnostic signal lives at the lesion margin and is destroyed by anything that restricts pooling rather than modulating it.

Stated limitation

The ROI protocol evaluates diagnosis given a localised lesion, not detection. Every ROI paper carries this, and it belongs in Limitations in one sentence rather than being defended.

Full mammograms are prepared and verified on disk — 2,089 training, 369 validation, 362 test, all resolving — so the whole-image extension is a concrete future direction rather than a hypothetical one. Estimated at five to nine weeks, with an expected outcome at or below the current numbers.

Superseded, kept for the record

An earlier phase used patient-grouped 5-fold CV with a seven-model greedy ensemble at a 0.70 sensitivity floor, reaching lesion AUC 0.9089 and 87.0% accuracy. It was retired in favour of the official-split single model because a single model on the published split is directly comparable to the literature, whereas a nested-selection ensemble on a custom protocol is not. The earlier numbers should not be mixed into the current tables.
